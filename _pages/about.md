👨‍🎓 Biography
======

Tongguan Wang, a fourth-year Ph.D. student at Huazhong Agricultural University, under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://faculty.hzau.edu.cn/sha/zh_CN/index.htm">Ying Sha</a>. He has published **9** papers as the first author and co-first author, including ACL, WWW, AAAI, EMNLP, etc. In addition, In addition, he assisted his supervisor in guiding undergraduate students to publish **3** papers.

💻 Research Interests
======
My research interests lie in **Natural Language Processing** and **Mutimodal Learning**, with focus on the following topics:

- **Multimodal Figurative Language Understanding** (e.g., Idioms, Sarcasm, and Memes)
- **Pragmatic Reasoning** (e.g., Non-literal and Context-dependent Communication)
- **Interpretability of LLMs and MLLMs** (e.g., Mechanistic Interpretability, Multimodal Reasoning)
